Homeward

Homeward · interactive prototype

Money travels home the way you do.

Every transfer is a flight: a route on the night map, a boarding pass for a receipt, a departures board for history.

  1. ✈
    Night map home screenArcs from your city to everyone you look after; sparks fly while money is in the air.
  2. ◐
    Marigold keypadFull-bleed amount entry with its own keypad, after Cash App. Type either side.
  3. ⇥
    Slide to sendDeliberate friction before money moves, then a takeoff.
  4. ▤
    Boarding-pass receiptsRoute, flight number, cargo and fare, with a flight log beneath.
  5. ☷
    Departures boardSplit-flap history: LANDED, IN FLIGHT, DELAYED.
  6. ◈
    Textile people cardsAdire, kente, beadwork and bandhani, generated per person.
  7. ☀
    Rate weatherA bento tile whose sky matches how today's rate compares with the month.
