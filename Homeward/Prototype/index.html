Homeward

Sending money home without the guesswork. This is a clickable version of the SwiftUI app; the pricing, validation and tracking rules match the tested Swift core.

What's different

  • No hidden markupMid-market rate, 0.4% margin and fee on every quote.
  • Rate locked for 30 minutesCountdown on the review screen.
  • Name check before you sendAccount checksum, then the account holder's name.
  • Live trackerTimestamps, ETA, free cancel until payout, automatic refunds.
  • Limits shown up frontNever halfway through a transfer.

Try this

  • Send £200 to Nigeria, then open the comparison.
  • Add a GTBank recipient with 2201456785 (wrong check digit), then fix it to 2201456784.
  • Use an account number containing 999 to see the name-mismatch warning.
  • Pay from balance, then cancel before delivery.
  • Open Mum's 70th birthday, add your share and send the pot home.
  • Open a delivered transfer and make a postcard.
  • Tap Your year home.
